# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Clipping operators managing several campaigns and social accounts from a Windows desktop. They choose campaigns, supply briefs and source videos, review finished clips, then publish and track them.

## Product Purpose

Turn a campaign entered once into repeatable video production, individual publication metadata, audience-compatible distribution, and traceable publishing. Each account serves one consistent audience scope. Campaign discovery and payments remain outside this workflow.

## Brand Personality

Retain the existing SSR_CLIPPER application identity. Use direct, practical Indonesian labels for new workflow controls, consistent with the user's language. No new brand direction has been requested.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboards that obscure work status, automatic changes after review, and opaque AI decisions. Existing controls and navigation are the visual reference.

## Design Principles

- Show missing information and errors where the user can act on them.
- Review the exact finished video and publication metadata that will be sent.
- Make campaign rules and account audience scope visible before production or distribution.
- Use templates for consistent editing while allowing evidence-based AI adaptation.
- Preserve completed work and make recovery explicit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use labelled native controls, keyboard-accessible actions, visible focus, readable status text in addition to color, and reduced-motion support. No additional user-specific accessibility requirement has been stated.
